whisper.cpp vs openai/whisper -- Measured Benchmark

base.en model, both engines, same machine (Apple Silicon Mac), same audio. Real measurements, not estimates.
Test audio: JFK inaugural clip, 11.0s, 22-word ground-truth transcript ("...ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country.").
Noise conditions: clean, and three synthetically-degraded variants (additive white noise at 5dB, 0dB, -5dB SNR) to get differentiated accuracy data -- the clean clip alone produced 0% WER on both engines, which doesn't distinguish them.
Raw engine numbers: isolated single-shot runs via /usr/bin/time -l (exact OS-level CPU/memory accounting), not estimated.
Live relay numbers: measured through the actual WebSocket relay servers used by the app (relays/whispercpp_relay.py, relays/openai_whisper_relay.py), 3 repeated trials each, real-time audio pacing.

Output duplicationHeavy -- same sentence re-emitted 6+ times with growing prefix, by design (sliding-window re-transcription)None -- one message per distinct utterance
Architecturally different strategies: whisper.cpp relay re-decodes a sliding window every 1.2s (fast first feedback, but restates text repeatedly -- client-side dedup needed). openai/whisper relay waits for a detected speech pause (VAD) before transcribing once per utterance (slower first feedback, zero duplication, stops promptly when speech stops).
